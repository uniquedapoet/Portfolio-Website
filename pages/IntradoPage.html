About My Time At Intrado

During my internship with Intrado’s network team, I developed an automated reporting system to replace the existing manual workflow. The system generated four distinct reports that previously took over six hours each to complete manually. I connected directly to internal databases to ensure data accuracy and reliability, and hosted the tool on a local server to enable seamless access across teams. By eliminating manual processes and optimizing resource use, the system will save the company approximately $10,000 in labor and unused resources annually.

Reports Automated

  • PSAP (Public Safety Answering Point) Impact Report: One of the largest reports handled by the network team. It includes information on every answering point that was serviced, modified, or "touched"—ensuring visibility for all involved stakeholders.
  • Traffic Study Report: Generated multiple times a week to isolate and analyze call routing to specific PSAPs.
  • Unused PSAP Report: A particularly impactful report that identifies active but unused PSAPs. These often represent unnecessary monthly costs— starting around $80 and increasing over time—potentially saving the company thousands of dollars by identifying lines that can be removed.
